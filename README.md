## Nick Wright

Senior Research Scientist at DPIRD in Western Australia, and Adjunct Research
Fellow at UWA. I build deep learning models for satellite imagery, and the
tooling around them: getting imagery in, running models over it at scale, and
turning the output into something usable.

Most of my work lives under [DPIRD-DMA](https://github.com/DPIRD-DMA) rather
than on this account. Ten tools, nine on PyPI, 800,000+ downloads, 900+ stars.
